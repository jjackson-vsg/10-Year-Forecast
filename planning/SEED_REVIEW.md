# Seed review — 16 September 2026

Scope: all four forecast narratives, repository documentation, dataset/export,
runtime/navigation code, styling structure, artwork metadata and generation prompts.
This is a desk review to commission research, not an independent external-source
audit or completed visual QA. Existing claims of historical checks are not new evidence.

## What is worth keeping

- A complete dependency-free, portable site with ten coherent annual illustrations.
- Thirty editorial cards already separate rationale, evidence, caveat and watch signals.
- Source type labels and timing windows improve on the original point-date narratives.
- Model comparison acknowledges correlated errors and unverified provider labels.
- The synthesis challenges unsupported crash magnitudes, elections, war dates,
  universal medical claims and the confusion of annual and long-term warming.
- Responsive styles, reduced-motion handling, dialog focus, URL state and history
  provide a useful starting point; verify them in a real browser before claiming quality.

## Research gaps and verification priorities

| Finding | Consequence | Required treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Four conversational forecasts, not six targeted research studies | Shared assumptions can masquerade as consensus | New domain research plus independent challenge; retain originals only as seed inputs |
| Eighteen sources carry thirty broad forecasts | A citation can be relevant without supporting the forecast's strength | Claim-level support matrix, precise locators, counterevidence and independent corroboration |
| Source record dates and titles are asserted in the seed | Plausible-looking citations may be wrong, inaccessible or superseded | Open original sources; verify publication and event dates, figures, definitions and revisions |
| High/Medium/Speculative mixes confidence and likelihood | A high-impact event can be uncertain without being unlikely | Separate event probability, evidence strength and timing uncertainty |
| Fixed three-events-per-year structure | Design can dictate content and imply scheduled events | Time-window-led register; annual chapters are editorial navigation |
| Model-specific percentages lack calibration | Quantitative appearance can exaggerate authority | Keep only as attributed raw judgements; define events before estimating new probabilities |
| Limited geography and institutional detail | Rich-country experience may be presented as global | UK/Europe, North America, China, India, LATAM and Africa coverage with explicit evidence gaps |
| Generic macro evidence attached to conflict and productivity claims | An economic outlook may not support causal or dated geopolitical assertions | Stronger domain sources; lower confidence or remove claim when unsupported |
| Scenarios are brief static paragraphs | Readers cannot see dependencies or decision consequences | Evidence-linked scenario cards and meaningful sensitivities, without fake simulation |
| No structured surprise register or outcome rules | Weak strategic preparedness and no future scoring | Threshold-defined events, signals, mechanisms, resolution criteria and revision log |
| Charts repeat hard-coded numbers in `app.js` | Updated data can leave stale labels, SVGs and accessible text | Move chart values/units/periods into canonical data; validate parity |
| UI renders several dataset strings as HTML | Future report ingestion could introduce markup or links unintentionally | Escape plain text; sanitise intentional rich content; never inject raw report HTML |
| No runnable baseline QA suite before migration | Documentation alone cannot demonstrate correct rendering | Add structural checks now; require browser, accessibility and accuracy checks later |

Review the personal-session anecdote about model refusals for relevance during S4;
it is not evidence about forecasting quality or censorship mechanisms. Provider labels
(including the Astra/Nova and Fable/Claude mismatches) must remain explicitly unverified.

Do not treat named 2026 events, regulatory approvals, agency forecasts or technical
performance numbers as confirmed merely because they occur in this seed. In particular,
check the precise IEA edition, WMO period/baseline, NASA mission definitions, FDA
indication/age range, NIST transition scope and any geopolitical probability attribution.

links, personal hosting URL, personal Git history and old revision identifiers are
not part of this version. Source citations, substantive forecast text and artwork
remain useful research inputs. Generic names of research tools are not account IDs.

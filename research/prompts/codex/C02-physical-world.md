You are conducting a rigorous deep research study for HORIZON, an engaging,
evidence-led guide to the future, with an optional strategy lens for a decentralised
vertical-software group such as Vesta. Research the next few years and the decade
2027–2036. The purpose is an informed view, not a confident-sounding story.
Use today's actual research date and state the information cutoff; do not pretend
to have read future publications. Separate current observed facts, measured estimates,
institutional projections, vendor/government targets and your own forecasts.

Browse original sources. Prefer primary papers/data, official statistics, regulators,
audited filings and independent evaluations; label credible secondary reporting when
needed. Seek counterevidence, base rates, failed deployments and independent source
families. Do not treat repeated coverage of one paper as independent corroboration.
Verify exact dates, units, geography, denominators and what each citation supports.
Do not invent citations, model identities, source contents or numbers. If inaccessible,
say so. Give source URLs and page/table/section locators, not only opaque citation markers.

Produce a substantial readable Markdown report, prioritising useful depth over a word
quota, with an executive summary and structured appendices. Target 12–20 resolvable
forecast questions, fewer if evidence is inadequate. Group them into 2027–2029,
2030–2032 and 2033–2036; use windows, not arbitrary milestones in every year.
Each forecast must define the event, population/geography, deadline, probability or
range only when justified (otherwise 'not quantified'), method/base rate, separate

Include central, faster-diffusion and constrained pathways, high-impact upside and
downside surprises, regional differences, and practical implications. A scenario is
not an assigned probability unless justified, mutually exclusive and exhaustive.
Cover UK/Europe, North America, China, India, Latin America and Africa where relevant;

No private internal company data is provided: do not invent it or financial uplift.
Use transparent illustrative assumptions for any strategy sensitivity.

unknown, scope/method/limits; (2) 5–8 key conclusions; (3) current baseline;

## Specific research assignment


Assess the physical constraints and enablers of 2027–2036: data-centre electricity,
grid connections, generation/storage, transformers, semiconductors, cooling/water,
critical minerals, nuclear/geothermal/fusion, electrification and physical automation.
Distinguish announced capacity, financed projects, connected capacity, reliable output
and affordable delivered energy. Compare editions and scenarios consistently.
For fusion separate plasma gain, plant-boundary net electricity and commercial economics.

For robotics compare conventional automation, mobile robots, humanoids and autonomy:
paid deployment, uptime, human interventions, safety, integration cost and payback,
controlled environments versus homes. Do not extrapolate demo videos into fleet economics.
For climate distinguish annual temperature exceedance from long-term thresholds;
study adaptation, insurability, heat/water stress and infrastructure resilience.
Include regional bottlenecks, deployment lead times, credible ranges and upside
learning curves. Explain feedback with AI demand without double counting forecasts.
Map consequences to software customers, operating reliability and product opportunities.
